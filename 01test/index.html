Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using the internet, computers, smartphones, and other digital technologies in a safe, responsible, and respectful way.

What is Digital Law?

Digital Law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a set of rules that explain how people are allowed to use technology, computers, networks, and the internet in a school, workplace, or organization.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is when you use someone else's words, ideas, images, or work and present them as your own without giving proper credit.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is cheating or using dishonest methods to gain an unfair advantage in school or college.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

Rules for AI-generated content are guidelines for using tools like ChatGPT responsibly, especially for schoolwork.
